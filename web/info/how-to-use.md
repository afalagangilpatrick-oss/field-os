# How to use Field OS

Field OS is the working system on the Dell Latitude 7212 Rugged Extreme tablet that Acme Home Services carries to every job. This page walks through it screen by screen, in the order the screens appear, with short numbered steps for each screen. It is written in plain words for the owner of a handy-person business, and for anyone the owner lets use the tablet on a job.

Everything on this page is already built into the tablet. No step needs extra software, a store download, or an account that was not already set up. When a screen has a button that is not described here yet, the button label says what it does, and the More info screen holds the longer pages about utilities and hardware.

Keep one habit everywhere: read the screen before tapping. Field OS never deletes anything on its own, so a tap can almost always be undone, but reading first is still the fastest way to work.

## Getting started and signing in

1. Press the power button on the edge of the tablet and wait for the screen to light up. Field OS starts by itself and stops at the login screen.
2. The login screen offers two ways in: Owner and Customer.
3. To sign in as Owner, tap Owner, then enter the PIN or the password that was set up for the owner account. The Owner login reaches every screen on the tablet, including the Terminal in Apps and the Private screen.
4. To sign in as Customer, tap Customer. The customer login is for the person whose home you are working in, and it only reaches the customer side screens described near the end of this page.
5. After signing in, the Home screen opens. To leave Field OS again, use the Exit button in the top bar described next.

## The top bar

The top bar is the strip that stays at the top of every screen.

1. On the left it shows the vertical "FIELD OS" label and the Exit button with a door icon.
2. In the middle it shows the logo.
3. On the right it shows the date and the time, the Owner tag when signed in as Owner, and the battery level.
4. To sign out, tap Exit with the door icon. That returns the tablet to the login screen without turning the tablet off.

## Quick settings

The quick settings drop-down lives in the top bar.

1. Tap the quick settings drop-down in the top bar to open it.
2. The drop-down holds the fast controls: volume, brightness, Wi-Fi, auto-lock, screen sleep, Bluetooth, rotation lock, keyboard, night light, airplane mode, and power.
3. Tap any one of them to switch it on or off, or drag its slider for volume and brightness.
4. Auto-lock and screen sleep are the two to check before handing the tablet to a customer, so the screen does not lock or dim while they are looking at it.
5. Airplane mode turns the radios off for work where Wi-Fi must stay off; tap it again to turn the radios back on.
6. Power opens the choices for shutting the tablet down or restarting it. Tap outside the drop-down to close it without changing anything.

## Home

The Home screen is the row of big buttons the owner works from all day.

1. After signing in as Owner you land on Home.
2. The buttons appear in this order: Customers, Prospects, Schedule, Sign & Send, Emails, Partners, Rates & Discounts, Bookkeeping (with Export), Edit documents (coming soon), Repair Guide, Materials, Utilities, Apps, Local Files, More info, Private.
3. Tap a button once to open that screen. Every screen has a way back to Home, usually the logo in the top bar.
4. Edit documents shows as coming soon; it is not needed for daily work yet.

## Customers

1. From Home, tap Customers to open the list of customers.
2. Tap a customer's name to open their record.
3. In the record, use the buttons to see the customer's jobs, their schedule entries, their signed documents, and their invoices.
4. To add a customer, use the add button on the Customers list, fill in the name and contact details, and save.
5. Customer details are used by the Welcome screen on the customer side, so spell the name the way the customer spells it.

## Prospects

1. From Home, tap Prospects for people who have asked for a quote but have not booked yet.
2. Tap a prospect to open their record and see the notes from the first conversation.
3. When a prospect books a job, move them from Prospects to Customers from their record, so the Schedule and Sign & Send screens can use them.
4. To add a prospect, use the add button on the Prospects list.

## Schedule

1. From Home, tap Schedule to see the calendar of booked jobs.
2. Tap a day to see the jobs for that day, with the customer name and the address.
3. Tap a job to open its details and change the time if the customer calls to move it.
4. To add a job, use the add button, pick the customer from the Customers list, set the date and time, and save.
5. The Schedule is the screen to check first every morning.

## Sign & Send

1. From Home, tap Sign & Send to prepare a quote, an agreement, or an invoice for signature.
2. Pick the customer, pick the document type, and fill in the lines for the work and the price.
3. Send it to the customer so they can sign on the customer side Sign screen.
4. Once it is signed, the signed copy is stored with the customer's record and shows up under Documents on the customer side.

## Emails

1. From Home, tap Emails to read and send the business email from the tablet.
2. Tap an email in the list to open it, and use the reply button to answer.
3. To write a new email, use the new-message button, pick a customer or prospect from the list, type, and send.
4. Quotes and invoices that were sent by email can also be found again from the customer's record.

## Partners

1. From Home, tap Partners to see the other businesses you work with, such as the licensed electrician, plumber, or gas fitter you refer work to.
2. Tap a partner to open their record with their phone number and notes about what they take.
3. Use the add button to add a new partner when you find a licensed professional you trust.

## Rates & Discounts

1. From Home, tap Rates & Discounts to set the hourly rate and the standard prices for common repairs.
2. Change a rate on this screen and the new rate is used on new quotes and invoices.
3. Set up any repeat customer discount here as well, so it is applied the same way every time.
4. Saved rates feed the Sign & Send screen, so check this screen before sending a first quote to a new customer.

## Bookkeeping and Export

1. From Home, tap Bookkeeping to see the money side of the business: invoices paid, invoices still open, and the ledger of the jobs.
2. Tap a line to see which job and which customer it belongs to.
3. To move the records somewhere else, use the Export button on the Bookkeeping screen. Export writes the bookkeeping data out as a file; it does not delete or change anything in Field OS.
4. Exported files land in Local Files under Documents, where the owner can copy them to a USB stick or upload them from Apps.
5. Nothing is ever deleted from the bookkeeping, so an Export is always a copy, never a move.

## Repair Guide

1. From Home, tap Repair Guide for the step-by-step guides for the repairs you do most often.
2. Pick a repair from the list to read its guide, with the materials from the Materials screen and the price from Rates & Discounts shown alongside.
3. Use the guide on the job to check the steps before starting, especially for work that may need a licensed trade where you work.

## Materials

1. From Home, tap Materials to manage materials and prices.
2. Materials stores the price sheets from Supplier A and Supplier B, so the price of a part is looked up instead of guessed.
3. To check what a part should cost, tap Compare: it shows the stored Supplier A and Supplier B prices side by side so the cheaper store is obvious.
4. To keep the prices current, tap Store price lists and pick the store sheet you want to save; the stored list is then used by Compare.
5. When a job needs a part, open Materials first, compare the stores, and put the chosen price into the quote on Sign & Send.

## Utilities

Utilities holds the tablet tools. From Home, tap Utilities, then tap the tool you need.

1. Password reset: for the tablet accounts, when a password is forgotten.
2. Camera and Gallery: take photos of the job and look at them again, which is what the customer Photos screen shows.
3. Calculator: quick math on the job.
4. Notepad: short notes; they also show up in Local Files under Notepad.
5. Room sketch: a simple sketch of the room for measurements.
6. Sound recorder: record a note or the sound of a problem.
7. Flashlight: uses the tablet light.
8. Card payment: explains that the smart card slot on the tablet cannot take card payments, and opens the card reader app or a payment app instead. Use one of those apps to take the money.
9. Maps & mileage: the route to the job and the miles for the bookkeeping.
10. Offline backup: saves everything as one .zip file, so it works with no Wi-Fi; copy the file to a USB stick or SD card to keep it off the tablet. See the Backups section at the end of this page.
11. Back up to cloud: saves a copy to the cloud drive account, which needs Wi-Fi. See Backups below.
12. Fingerprint reader: saves a finger and tests it. Signing in with a finger is not switched on yet, so the PIN or password is still needed.
13. Keyboard: sets the on-screen keyboard key size (small, medium, large, xlarge) and keyboard height (a percent of the screen). Bigger keys help with gloves on.
14. Batteries: shows the charge of the two tablet batteries.
15. Card reader: the smart card slot and contactless reader, for ID and access cards (not for payments and not for camera memory cards).
16. Dock & screens: how the tablet behaves when docked at the shop.
17. GPS location: shows where the tablet is, on models with the GPS option.
18. Update OS: installs the bigger system updates. See the Updates section at the end of this page.

## Apps

1. From Home, tap Apps to open the apps, each in its own window.
2. The apps are: Assistant, Chat assistant, Search assistant, Browser, Documents, Cloud drive, Pay app, Wallet, Payments, Bank, Social, Neighborhood, Photo social, Leads, Short video, and Terminal.
3. Card payments go through the card reader app (from the Card payment utility) or the payment apps (Pay app, Wallet, Payments), because the smart card slot cannot take payments, as the Card payment utility explains.
4. Terminal is owner only: it does not appear when the tablet is signed in as Customer. The Terminal is where the Private screen is unlocked after three wrong answers, as described in the Private section below.
5. Tap the app icon to open it; the top bar stays available so Exit and quick settings still work.

## Local Files

1. From Home, tap Local Files to browse the files on the tablet, like a file manager on a desktop computer.
2. The root holds the folders Documents, Downloads, Notepad, Pictures, Videos, Sounds, and Programs.
3. Tap a folder to open it, and tap a file to open it in the right app.
4. Switch between Icons view and Details view with the view button, to see file sizes and dates or just the icons.
5. Files you Export from Bookkeeping, save from Notepad, or take with the Camera land in these folders, most often Documents, Pictures, or Notepad.

## More info

1. From Home, tap More info for the reading pages.
2. The pages are: utilities and hardware, and this how-to page.
3. Check your own state's rules before a job that might need a licensed electrician, plumber, or gas fitter.
4. The utilities and hardware page describes every Field OS utility and the parts of the tablet itself.

## Private

1. From Home, tap Private. Private holds what customers "deleted", so nothing is really gone.
2. The first time, tap Create password and set the Private password.
3. If the password is forgotten, tap Forgot password: it asks for the launch date of the business. Answer with the launch date to get back in.
4. Three wrong answers lock Private. While it is locked, it can only be unlocked from the Terminal in Apps (owner only), or by the assistant on the tablet, as the lock message says.
5. To put something back, open Private and tap Re-add next to the item. The item returns to where it was, marked with a red * so it is clear it came back from Private.

## Customer side

The customer side is what the person whose home you are working in sees when they sign in as Customer.

1. Welcome: the customer is looked up by name, so spell the name right in Customers.
2. Sign: where the customer signs the quotes and agreements you sent from Sign & Send.
3. Photos: the photos of the job from the Camera.
4. Invoices: the invoices for the work, including the ones already paid.
5. Documents: the signed documents stored with the customer's record.
6. Customers cannot reach the owner screens from the customer side, so handing the tablet over for signing is safe.

## Updates and Update OS

1. Small design changes install by themselves while the tablet is on and connected.
2. Bigger updates wait for you: they are installed from the Update OS button in Utilities.
3. When a bigger update is waiting, tap Update OS in Utilities and let the tablet finish. Do not power the tablet off while it runs.
4. After the update the tablet restarts into the new version of Field OS, and the screens described on this page stay in the same order.

## Backups

1. Two backups are built in, both from Utilities.
2. Offline backup saves everything as one .zip file, so it works with no Wi-Fi, for example in a basement with no signal. Copy the file to a USB stick or SD card so a lost tablet does not take the backup with it.
3. Back up to cloud saves a copy to the cloud drive, which needs Wi-Fi and the Cloud drive app from Apps signed in.
4. Do both: the Offline backup for the job site, the cloud backup for the shop.
5. Nothing is ever deleted when backing up, and a backup is always a copy.
